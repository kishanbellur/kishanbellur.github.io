Our paper [Slug Flow Condensation Identification with Acoustic Signatures](/publication/yan_2026) is now published in the *International Journal of Heat and Mass Transfer*.

Two-phase flows move a lot of heat, but they can change regime abruptly. In real equipment, the pipes and chambers are rarely transparent, so high-speed imaging isn't available for monitoring. This paper shows that the acoustic signature of condensing flow carries enough information to identify slug flow. It points toward cheap, non-intrusive sensors that can report what is happening inside closed hardware in real time.

This builds on our earlier work on acoustic signatures of boiling critical heat flux (APS-DFD 2023) and flow condensation (Gordon Research Conference 2025). Read more on the [acoustic diagnostics research page](/research/acoustics).
