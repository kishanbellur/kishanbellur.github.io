UCLID had a strong showing at the [10th Thermal and Fluids Engineering Conference (ASTFE)](https://www.astfe.org/tfec2025/).

**First place in the Green Energy CFD Competition.** [Amirhosein Sarchami](/people/amirhoseinsarchami) led a team that won **1st place** in the inaugural [ASTFE Green Energy CFD Competition](https://www.astfe.org/tfec2025/first-annual-astfe-green-energy-cfd-competition/). Simulation is at the center of how our group works, and this was a good test of our students' ability to apply it to an open-ended, real-world energy problem under competition conditions.

**Two technical talks:**
* Amirhosein presented *"Leveraging Temperature-dependent Wettability to Control Flow in Porous Media"*, our work on using thermally responsive surfactants to switch capillary flow regimes ([more on porous transport](/research/PorousTransport)).
* [Ayaaz Yasin](/people/ayaazyasin) presented *"Exploring Two-Dimensional Flows in Evaporating Thin Films: A Step Towards a Dynamic Model"*, which extends our steady thin-film models to capture 2D flow near the contact line ([more on thin films](/research/thinfilm)).
