UCLID traveled to ASME IMECE 2025 with three talks, all on our thin-film and multiscale phase change work:

* *"A Coupled Multiscale Study of Phase Change Dynamics at Curved Liquid-Vapor Interfaces"*
* *"Dynamic Measurement of Evaporating Liquid Films"*
* *"Multiscale Oscillations in Thin Liquid Films"*

Together, these talks show where the [thin-film program](/research/thinfilm) is heading: from steady models of an evaporating meniscus to **dynamic** models and measurements that capture oscillations, contact line motion, and the onset of instability.

[Amirhosein](/people/amirhoseinsarchami) received an **NSF-sponsored travel award** to attend and present, and was joined by labmates [Unmeelan](/people/unmeelanchakrabarti) and [Saaras](/people/saaraspakanati).

At the same meeting, I was honored to be recognized as an ASME **Rising Star of Mechanical Engineering**. Thank you to my students, collaborators, and mentors who made this possible.
