We are honored to receive the 2025 **Gold Star Lab Award** from UC's College of Engineering and Applied Science.

This award reflects the students' work. Over the past year, lab members have defended PhD and MS theses, published in *Applied Thermal Engineering*, *Colloids and Surfaces A*, and *Nanoscale and Microscale Thermophysical Engineering*, won competitive fellowships, and presented at venues from APS-DFD to NASA's TFAWS. The same week, [Saaras Pakanati](/people/saaraspakanati) was selected for a [2025 Armstrong Fellowship for Discovery](https://research.uc.edu/armstrong-institute). It's great to see undergraduate researchers recognized alongside our graduate students.

Thank you to everyone in UCLID for making this such a productive and supportive group.
