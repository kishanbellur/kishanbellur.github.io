UCLID students have collected a remarkable set of competitive awards over the past several months:

* **[Amirhosein Sarchami](/people/amirhoseinsarchami)** received the prestigious *Dean's Dissertation Completion Fellowship* (April 2026).
* **[Saaras Pakanati](/people/saaraspakanati)** received the *Merchant M. Eugene Scholarship* (April 2026). Earlier, in December 2025, he became a two-time [Armstrong Fellow](https://research.uc.edu/armstrong-institute), an unusual distinction for an undergraduate researcher.
* **[Ayaaz Yasin](/people/ayaazyasin)** won the competitive *University Research Council (URC) fellowship* (February 2026) and a *Graduate Student Government Research Fellowship Award* (March 2026).
* **[Emmett Looman](/people/emmettlooman)** received the *Robert Dorsey Scholarship* from the College of Engineering and Applied Science.

These awards span every career stage in the lab, from undergraduate researchers to PhD candidates finishing their dissertations. Congratulations to all!
